Entry for the [One Star for Monday](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-one-star-for-monday-9ead4a61) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib) writes a single line of plain text — a one-star
review of Monday, written as though Monday were a restaurant — to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Came for brunch, got Monday instead: cold coffee, a table by the printer, and a bill charging me for the whole week.

22 words, 116 characters, one line. The table, the dish and the bill all go
wrong, and the joke only works for Monday: you came for brunch, got seated at
Monday instead, and the bill charges you for the whole week.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
